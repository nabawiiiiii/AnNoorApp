__Description__ :

🌟 Noor AI – Your Intelligent Islamic Companion

Noor AI is a next-generation Islamic application powered by artificial intelligence, designed to bring you closer to the Qur’an and Sunnah in your daily life.
Whether you seek knowledge, guidance, or inspiration, Noor AI illuminates your spiritual path with wisdom and simplicity.

✨ Main Features

🧠 AI Islamic Assistant – Ask any question about Islam and receive accurate answers with authentic references from the Qur’an and Hadith.

📖 Qur’an Study Mode – Read, listen, and understand the Qur’an with tafsir explanations and audio recitations by Yasser Al-Dossari.

🕋 Daily Spiritual Plan – Get a personalized program for prayer, dhikr, Qur’an recitation, and reflection.

📚 Authentic Knowledge Base – Verified Islamic sources (Ibn Kathir, Al-Saadi, Al-Jalalayn, and more).

🌙 Beautiful Islamic Interface – Elegant design inspired by Islamic art, with gold and green accents.

🔔 Smart Reminders – Receive gentle reminders for Salah, dhikr, and good deeds.

💤 Night Mode – Read comfortably at night with an eye-friendly dark theme.

🧭 Offline Mode – Access Qur’an and tafsir even without internet.


🌍 Our Mission

To merge technology and faith — helping Muslims around the world learn, reflect, and live by the light of revelation, anytime and anywhere.
